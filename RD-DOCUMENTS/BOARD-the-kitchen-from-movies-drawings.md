## The plan (PR 1 of the kitchen)

- Each piece its own FIXTURE kind on the KITCHEN row: *"the SINK should be
  seperate from the Dishwasher / Drawers etc. unless joined in the KITCHEN
  layout"*.
- WHERE TWO COUNTER PIECES MEET -- base, sink base, drawers, door base, DW,
  range -- on the same face of the same wall, the end they share is one
  light line (*"a lightweight line at the joint to distinguish"*), in plan.
  A piece pressed within 6" of another slides flush against it, so there is
  a joint to meld; it never slides into one.
- In SECTION each piece stands in Movie's front or side elevation
  (`fixture-profiles.js`); a CABINET run is 24" door bases side by side, as
  many bays as the run holds. One line at a joint, the same as plan.

# RoughDrafter LITE — "The Drag Race" (30s spot)

**Tagline: ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES.**

Paste the SHOT LIST below into ChatGPT (or any image tool) one shot at a time to
generate storyboard frames, then hand the frames + the TIMING table to a video
generator. Style notes and prompts are included per shot.

---

## LOGLINE

A drafter in an office chair lines up against a sports car at a drag strip.
The light drops green. The car launches — but the drafter calmly draws a house
outline, hits one button, and a full 3D house grows out of his screen. He's
standing at the finish line holding a glowing hologram of the finished house
before the car crosses.

## GLOBAL STYLE (put this at the top of every ChatGPT prompt)

> Cinematic storyboard frame, widescreen 16:9, dusk drag strip lit by amber
> sodium lights, light haze, high contrast, slightly gritty film look.
> Recurring characters: (1) THE DRAFTER — calm 40s man in a plaid shirt,
> sitting in a rolling office chair at a small drafting desk with a laptop,
> coffee mug on the desk; (2) THE CAR — low aggressive orange sports car,
> heat shimmer off the hood. Keep both characters consistent across frames.

---

## SHOT LIST

**SHOT 1 — THE LINEUP (0:00–0:04)**
Wide shot, straight down the drag strip. In the left lane: the orange sports
car, engine idling, driver in a helmet gripping the wheel. In the right lane:
the drafter in his office chair at his little desk, laptop open, totally
relaxed, taking a sip of coffee. Christmas-tree start lights glowing amber
between them.
*Prompt add-on: "absurd deadpan contrast, the office desk looks tiny next to
the race car."*

**SHOT 2 — THE STARE-DOWN (0:04–0:07)**
Close on the driver's eyes through the visor, flicking sideways in disbelief
at the drafter. Cut/split to the drafter giving a polite little nod, cracking
his knuckles over the keyboard. Start lights counting down amber-amber-amber.

**SHOT 3 — GREEN LIGHT (0:07–0:09)**
Extreme close-up of the tree slamming to GREEN, reflected in both the car's
windshield and the drafter's glasses. The car ERUPTS off the line, tire smoke
everywhere. The drafter… turns to his laptop.

**SHOT 4 — THE OUTLINE (0:09–0:14)**
Over-the-shoulder on the laptop screen: a clean white drafting canvas. His
cursor clicks corner-corner-corner — a bright neon-green house outline snaps
together, crisp 90° lines like a T-square is guiding them, dimension strings
popping onto every edge automatically. In the background lane, the sports car
is a blur streaking away.
*Prompt add-on: "screen shows minimalist CAD app, neon green outline on white,
architectural dimension strings."*

**SHOT 5 — ONE BUTTON (0:14–0:17)**
Macro close-up: his finger hits a single big button on screen labeled
**BUILD HOUSE**. Beat. The outline EXTRUDES — walls rise, floors stack,
a roof folds over the top like paper — a whole 3D white-clay house model
assembling itself in one continuous motion.
*Prompt add-on: "time-lapse feel, house self-assembling from a floor plan,
white clay 3D render style."*

**SHOT 6 — THE OVERTAKE (0:17–0:21)**
Side tracking shot down the strip: the sports car at full scream — and the
DRAFTER'S CHAIR rockets past it, the drafter still seated, one hand on the
laptop lid, papers streaming behind him like a contrail. Deadpan face.
*Prompt add-on: "impossible comedic speed, motion blur, office chair
overtaking a race car."*

**SHOT 7 — THE FINISH LINE (0:21–0:26)**
The finish line. The drafter is already there, standing beside his chair,
casually holding a glowing blue HOLOGRAM of the finished house floating above
his palm — walls, roof, little garage, all rotating slowly. The sports car
blasts through the beam behind him, driver's head whipping around in
disbelief. Checkered flag waves.
*Prompt add-on: "hologram house projected from hand, volumetric blue light,
race car losing in the background."*

**SHOT 8 — TITLE CARD (0:26–0:30)**
The hologram house spins alone on black, then snaps into a flat white logo
frame:
**ROUGHDRAFTER LITE**
**ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES.**
Small line underneath: *Draw the outline. Build the house.*
*(Optional last beat: a tiny dog trots across the bottom of the frame and
sits. Say nothing about it.)*

## HOW TO USE

1. Paste GLOBAL STYLE + one SHOT into ChatGPT → ask for a storyboard frame.
   Repeat for all 8 shots (keep the same chat so characters stay consistent).
2. Feed the 8 frames + TIMING TABLE into a video generator (Sora, Runway,
   Veo, Kling) shot by shot, using each shot's text as the motion prompt.
3. Cut the 8 clips together in order; drop the VO on top if you're using it.

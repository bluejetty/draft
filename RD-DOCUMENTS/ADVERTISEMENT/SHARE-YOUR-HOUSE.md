# SHARE YOUR HOUSE — the owners advertise for us

Not a video ad — a built-in marketing loop. Board #232.

## The moment

Two doors into the same sheet:

- **The bone's payoff** — when BUILD HOUSE finishes, the feature sheet
  comes out with the house: real-estate 3D front rendering up top, the
  2D plans (main floor and 2nd) below.
- **The cooldown** — while the hourly bone recharges, the app offers a
  SHARE moment: snapshots of their plans, or the same one-page sheet.

## The sheet

One page, real-estate style: 3D front view up top, floor plans below,
address and areas from the PROJECT page.

One tap, one page, ready to show off.

## The stamp

The Rough Drafter logo sits quietly in the corner of every sheet, like a
titleblock stamp. The plan sells the house; the stamp sells the app.

## The loop

1. They design the house.
2. They share the sheet to show it off — family, buyers, contractors,
   the group chat.
3. Every page carries the stamp.

Their pride is our marketing — the best kind, because it's real work by
real people. No ad budget spent; the cooldown does the selling.

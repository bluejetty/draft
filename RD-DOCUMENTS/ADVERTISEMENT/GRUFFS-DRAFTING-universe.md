# GRUFFS DRAFTING — the fast-food universe

The recurring fictional restaurant where dogs serve house plans like
burgers. Formerly workshopped as "McRuffDrafter" — renamed GRUFFS
DRAFTING so no video generator gets pulled toward any real fast-food
brand. There is no "Mc" anywhere in this universe.

## The joint

**GRUFFS DRAFTING** — a classy 1950s drive-through at heart. The flagship
look is Googie/populuxe: swooping canopy roof, chrome and pastel, big
neon script sign reading GRUFFS DRAFTING, terrazzo, starburst clock,
speaker posts at each stall. The dogs run it; they are the only competent
professionals in frame, and they play it dead straight.

Menu board staples: **THE BUNGALOW MEAL**, **TWO-STOREY COMBO**,
**THE RENO SPECIAL**. Orders come out as rolled plans with a rubber-band
snap. Payment is bones.

## The eras (different ads, different decades, same joint)

Gruffs has been quietly correcting plans since the fifties, so each ad
can visit a different era of the same franchise — its own look without
rebuilding the universe:

- **1950s — THE CARHOP era**: the covered canopy bay, cars angled in,
  roller-skate carhop girls gliding out with window-ledge trays. The
  classy original.
- **1970s — wood-panel era**: burnt-orange booths, wood panelling,
  smoked-glass pendant lights.
- **1980s — neon lobby era**: ketchup-red booths, backlit menu board,
  fluorescent hum. THE MANAGER and THE DEN live here.
- **Modern era**: sleek double-lane drive-thru, digital menu boards,
  same dogs, same deadpan competence.

## Ad concept — THE CARHOP (1950s spot)

Dusk under the neon. Cars angled beneath the swooping canopy. A driver
leans out and orders into the chrome speaker post: "One bungalow...
extra bedrooms." Inside the glass kitchen, dogs in paper hats work fast
and surgical over a drafting counter. A carhop girl on roller skates
glides out with a chrome tray, hooks it on the window ledge: a rolled
plan, a titleblock sheet, and a bone. The driver unrolls it — it's
perfect. The carhop spins away to the next car. Neon sign buzzes:
GRUFFS DRAFTING. Announcer: "Gruffs Drafting. Drive through. Drive home."

## BRAND SAFETY — the standing rule (all ads, all universes)

Every image/video generation prompt in this folder carries this rule,
born of a real accident (a generator put golden arches on the uniforms):

- NO real-world company logos, trademarks, or lookalike marks anywhere
  in frame — not on uniforms, hats, name tags, cups, wrappers, trays,
  signage, vehicles, or background buildings. No golden arches, no
  crowns, no swooshes, nothing recognizable.
- The only marks that exist in these worlds are our own fictional
  identities: GRUFFS DRAFTING, ROUGHDRAFTER / RUFFDRAFTER.
- Any generated output where a real brand sneaks in is REJECTED — never
  published. Check the uniforms and the background props before calling
  a take good.
- Avoid brand-adjacent naming in prompts ("Mc" + restaurant drags the
  burger aesthetic in with it) — original names confuse the model less
  than guardrails restrain it.

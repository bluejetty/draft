# GRUFFS DRAFTING — "THE MANAGER" (:60 TV SPOT)

*Same universe as THE DRIVE-THRU. Shot on film, neon dusk outside, fluorescent
hum inside. The comedy rule holds: the dogs are the only competent
professionals in frame, and they play it dead straight.*

---

**INTERIOR — GRUFFS DRAFTING LOBBY. DAY.**
Ketchup-red booths. A menu board glowing overhead: **THE BUNGALOW MEAL**,
**TWO-STOREY COMBO**, **THE RENO SPECIAL**. A family quietly eats fries while
their plan prints. All is well.

**THE DOORS BANG OPEN.**
A woman marches in — sunglasses on her head, a rolled-up house plan gripped
like a rolled-up newspaper. She slaps it on the counter.

**KAREN:**
"I want to speak to the MANAGER."

**COUNTER DOG** (border collie, paper visor, headset) doesn't flinch.
He taps a bone-shaped bell once. *Ding.*

**FROM THE BACK:** a golden retriever pushes through the swinging kitchen
doors. Paper manager's hat. Name tag: **MGR**. He puts both paws up on
the counter. Direct eye contact. Waiting.

**KAREN (unrolling the plan violently):**
"My contractor says my laundry room is in the CRAWLSPACE. My stairs hit a
BEAM. And don't get me STARTED on the—"

**WHILE SHE TALKS —** two dogs have already slid down the counter to the
plan. One pins the corner with a paw. The other walks the drawing with a
red pen in his teeth. They work FAST. Quiet. Surgical.

**INSERT — THE PLAN:** the laundry room slides upstairs. The stair opening
glides off the beam — the beam politely moves over for it. A missing
column appears at the beam end with a little *pop*. Dimensions re-string
themselves like birds landing on a wire.

**KAREN (still going, not looking down):**
"—and the window in the bedroom faces the NEIGHBOR'S window, which is
frankly a LAWSUIT, and my mother-in-law says—"

**INSERT — THE PLAN:** the bedroom window hops to the far wall. A closet
blooms beside it. The plan sparkles, brand new.

**MANAGER DOG** slides the corrected plan back across the counter. Rolls
it. The rubber band SNAPS on in slow-mo.

**KAREN (mid-sentence, picking it up, reading):**
"...and further— ...huh."
*(beat. squints. flips it over. flips it back.)*
"...This is... actually better."

**MANAGER DOG (deadpan, to camera):**
*"You want dims with that?"*

**KAREN (small voice):**
"...Yes please."

**THE WHOLE LOBBY** — the family, the counter dog, a dog mopping in the
back — all look at camera at once. Beat. The mop dog nods slowly.

**ANNOUNCER (V.O., huge):**
"Gruffs Drafting! Where every complaint... comes back CORRECTED!"

**JINGLE (kids choir, way too enthusiastic):**
🎵 *"Rough-Rough-RuffDrafter — the house you're dreamin' of!
Even when you're barkin' mad, it's ready with a WOOF!"* 🎵

**END CARD.** Neon grid horizon. The dog-bone "R" spins in chrome.
**RUFFDRAFTER** — *"Draw the outline. We do the rest."*

**ANNOUNCER (V.O., fast, small print voice):**
"Manager is a dog. All managers are dogs. Complaints resolved in under
two minutes or your dims are free."

in the foreground, the two counter dogs fix the plan in real time. She
grabs it, stops cold — "...huh." Bark. Logo.*

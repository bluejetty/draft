# RoughDrafter LITE — Standard End Clip (reusable, ~10s card + legal scroll)

One clip to bolt onto the end of every LITE ad. Two parts: the LOGO CARD (the full 10-second "meat"), then the LEGAL SCROLL (fine print, long, deadpan — real and funny at the same time; everything in it is actually true).

## Part 1 — Logo card (10 full seconds)

Chrome 1980s tumbling logo on a neon grid horizon with the big striped synthwave sun, light VHS grain:

> **ROUGHDRAFTER LITE**
> **ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!**
> **(or it's free)**
> *The Paw Knows.*

The → is a fun cartoon chrome arrow with a little speed-line whoosh — it does the "TO" work. If the generator garbles the arrow glyph, build the card text in the editor instead (CapCut/Resolve) over a generated background.

Slow logo shimmer/rotate the whole 10 seconds — long enough to reuse under any announcer read or jingle tail.

### Video-generator prompt (paste-ready)

Semi-realistic 1980s airbrushed-illustration style, halfway between photoreal and cartoon: painted-poster look, oversaturated sunset colors, light VHS grain. A chrome 1980s logo card holds on screen for the full duration: giant chrome letters reading "ROUGHDRAFTER LITE" over "ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!" with smaller text "(or it's free)" and small script text "The Paw Knows." beneath, floating over a neon grid horizon with a big striped synthwave sun, slow shimmer and gentle rotation on the chrome, subtle VHS tracking lines. No other text, no people, no scene numbers, no timestamps, no watermarks.

## Part 2 — Legal scroll (append in the edit, not generated)

Tiny white fine print on black, scrolling slightly too fast to read comfortably (the joke), deadpan legal-notice music-free or with the synth pad fading out. Build this in CapCut/Resolve as scrolling text over black — generators butcher small text.

### Scroll timing

- ~12–15 seconds of scroll at "slightly too fast" speed — readable only if you pause, which is the gag AND the standard industry practice.
- Opening sentence (the shack line) can hold 1 second larger/centered before the scroll starts, so viewers catch the joke, then the rest rolls.

## Reuse

Drag-race, THE PAW, mystery cut, THE SECRET DRAFTER — same end clip on all of them. Only the announcer read over Part 1 changes per ad; Part 2 never changes (that's how running gags compound: freeze-framers get rewarded with new discoveries every ad).

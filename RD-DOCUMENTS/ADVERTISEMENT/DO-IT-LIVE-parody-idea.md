# "WE'LL DO IT LIVE" — parody ad idea (stored 2026-08)

Source gag: the Bill O'Reilly outtake meltdown — "WE'LL DO IT LIVE! DO IT LIVE!
I CAN'T READ IT, THERE'S NO WORDS THERE!" — reborn as a RoughDrafter LITE spot.

## Premise
A frazzled drafter at a desk fighting a clunky, over-complicated CAD screen
(dialogs, ribbons, cryptic errors). The meltdown builds:

- "I CAN'T READ IT... THERE'S NO WORDS THERE!" (squinting at a microscopic
  toolbar / incomprehensible dialog)
- Slams the desk. Papers fly.
- Beat. He sees the paw button.
- "WE'LL DO IT LIVE!" — presses the paw.
- BUILD HOUSE fires: outline → walls, footings, roof, dims materialize on air.
- Calm settles. He leans back.
- End card: ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES! / standard endcard.

## Why it fits the brand
- An ad ABOUT losing it, from the company whose motto forgives losing it —
  A FEW MISTAKES ARE EXPECTED.
- The meltdown is the "before"; the paw is the "after". Same skeleton as the
  drag race: chaos build-up → one press → payoff.
- Parody/meme format travels on Reels/Shorts/TikTok; the quote is instantly
  recognizable to the 35+ demo who remember the clip.

## Notes
- Keep the payoff (house building itself) in the first 2 seconds on the
  short cut — hook-first lesson from the drag race retention dip.
- No need to imitate O'Reilly's likeness; the LINE is the meme. A generic
  fed-up drafter avoids any likeness issue.
- Candidate tagline pairing: "WE'LL DO IT LIVE." → "ROUGHLY TRUE. NEVER
  PERFECT."
- Origin: user coined it while discussing tuning the foundation-height fade
  convention live on real plans — "we will do it live %^%^% it we'll do it
  live."

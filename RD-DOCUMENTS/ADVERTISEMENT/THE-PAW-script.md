## The spot

**Shot 1 (0–3s) — The discovery.**
A mustached drafter in a plaid shirt sits at his desk working in a clean drafting program on a retro CRT monitor. Quiet room, desk lamp — through the window behind him, the big striped synthwave sun sits on a neon grid horizon, sunset glow washing the room in orange and purple. He notices a small, unexplained PAW-PRINT button in the corner of the screen. He leans in, curious, and murmurs: "...what's this?" His finger presses it.

**Shot 2 (3–7s) — The eye.**
A small round camera lens centered on top of his monitor glows on — a calm red eye. Cut to the EYE'S VIEW: red-tinted fisheye of the drafter's face looking into the lens, curious, lit by the screen glow. A calm, gentle computer voice says:

> "Good afternoon, Dave. You pressed the paw. Do you do this... of your own free will?"

The drafter raises an eyebrow. Beat.

**Shot 3 (7–10s) — Hard cut to the logo card.**
Chrome 1980s tumbling logo on a neon grid horizon, synthwave sun:

> **ROUGHDRAFTER LITE — ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!**

Announcer (fast, 80s toy-commercial energy): "RoughDrafter LITE! Zero to plan in five minutes!"

No answer to HAL's question. No explanation of the paw. That's the whole joke — the ad just moves on and never mentions it again.

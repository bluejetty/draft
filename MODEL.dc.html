Layer Philosophy
  • Layers follow AIA-style CAD names (A-WALL-EXT, S-SLAB…) so drawings convert cleanly to AutoCAD/DXF.
  • Commands assign layers automatically — the layers organize themselves, so nobody manages them by hand.

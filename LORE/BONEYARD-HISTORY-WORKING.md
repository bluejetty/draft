Verify the exact ritual wording, earliest dated appearance (early exposures like *Masonry Dissected*, Samuel Prichard, 1730, may contain it), and how universal the no-solicitation rule is/was. This is the historical anchor for the BONEYARD's voluntary-discovery principle: nobody is funneled in, whoever knocks, knocks.

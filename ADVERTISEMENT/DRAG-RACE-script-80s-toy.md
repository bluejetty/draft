# RoughDrafter LITE — "The Drag Race" (80s TOY COMMERCIAL CUT, 30s)

**Tagline: ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!**

Same race, new decade: shot like a 1986 Saturday-morning toy commercial —
VHS grain, neon chrome, a screaming announcer, and kids losing their minds.
Paste GLOBAL STYLE + one SHOT at a time into ChatGPT for storyboard frames,
then feed frames + TIMING into your video tool.

---

## LOGLINE

A drafter in an office chair lines up against a hot orange sports car —
toy-commercial style. Green light! The car launches, the ANNOUNCER SCREAMS,
the drafter draws a house outline, SLAMS the BUILD HOUSE button, and blasts
past the car to the finish line holding a glowing hologram house while kids
gasp. "ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!"

## GLOBAL STYLE (top of every prompt)

> 1980s toy-commercial aesthetic: shot on grainy VHS videotape, slight tracking
> lines, oversaturated colors, hard studio lighting mixed with dusk drag strip,
> neon grid horizon, chrome-and-laser-beam graphics, comic-book zoom lines.
> Recurring characters: (1) THE DRAFTER — calm 40s man in a plaid shirt in a
> rolling office chair at a tiny drafting desk with a chunky retro laptop,
> coffee mug; (2) THE CAR — low aggressive orange 80s wedge sports car with
> pop-up headlights, heat shimmer; (3) TWO KIDS — wide-eyed 10-year-olds in
> striped shirts watching from the guardrail. Keep everyone consistent.

## SHOT LIST

**SHOT 1 — THE LINEUP (0:00–0:04)**
Wide VHS shot down the strip: wedge car revving in the left lane, drafter
sipping coffee at his tiny desk in the right lane. Vertical drag-race
starting light stack (amber-amber-green) between them. Big chrome letters
SLAM in from the sides: "THE RACE OF THE CENTURY!"
*Announcer (screaming): "IT'S THE RACE OF THE CENTURYYY!"*

**SHOT 2 — THE KIDS (0:04–0:07)**
Cut to the two kids at the guardrail, leaning in, mouths open. One elbows
the other and points at the drafter. Zoom lines behind their heads.
*Announcer: "A REAL RACE CAR… VERSUS A GUY WITH A LAPTOP?!"*

**SHOT 3 — GREEN LIGHT (0:07–0:09)**
Extreme close-up: the light stack drops amber-amber-GREEN, reflected in the
driver's visor and the drafter's glasses. The car ERUPTS in tire smoke.
Freeze-frame on the smoke with a comic "GO!!!" burst graphic.

**SHOT 4 — THE OUTLINE (0:09–0:14)**
Over-the-shoulder on the chunky laptop: neon-green house outline snaps
together corner-corner-corner on a white canvas, dimension strings popping
on. Each click gets an 80s laser "PEW" flash. The car streaks past behind him.
*Announcer: "HE DRAWS THE HOUSE… HIMSELF?! NO WAY!"*

**SHOT 5 — THE BUTTON (0:14–0:17)**
Macro shot: finger SLAMS a huge red arcade-style button labeled BUILD HOUSE.
Screen flash. The outline extrudes into a full 3D white-clay house — walls up,
roof folding over — with starburst glints on every corner.
*Announcer: "ONE! BUTTON! BUILDS! THE HOUSE!"*
*Kids (in unison): "WHOAAAA!"*

**SHOT 6 — THE OVERTAKE (0:17–0:21)**
Side tracking shot: the office chair ROCKETS past the race car, papers
streaming like a contrail, neon speed lines painted over the frame,
cheap-video star wipe chasing him. Deadpan drafter face.
*Announcer: "HE'S GOT THE POWER OF ROUGHDRAFTER LIIIITE!"*

**SHOT 7 — THE FINISH (0:21–0:26)**
Finish line: drafter already standing there, hologram house rotating over his
palm, checkered flag waving, kids jumping and high-fiving in the foreground.
The car crosses second, driver slumped. Confetti. Freeze-frame.
*Announcer: "FINISHED… BEFORE THE CAR! IS THAT EVEN LEGAL?!"*

**SHOT 8 — TITLE CARD (0:26–0:30)**
The hologram snaps onto a black starfield. Chrome 3D logo tumbles in and
lands with a boom, laser underline sweep:
**ROUGHDRAFTER LITE**
**ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!**
Fast-talking fine print at the bottom. A tiny dog trots across, sits, and
stares at the camera. Say nothing about it.
*Announcer: "ROUGHDRAFTER LITE! ZERO TO PLAN IN FIVE MINUTES!"*
*(Kid whisper, off-mic:) "…so cool."*

## HOW TO USE

1. Paste GLOBAL STYLE + one SHOT into ChatGPT → storyboard frame. Same chat
   for all 8 so the characters and VHS look stay consistent.
2. Feed frames + TIMING into the video tool shot by shot; each shot's text is
   the motion prompt. Ask for "VHS tape wobble and tracking lines" in every clip.
3. Cut together, add the announcer VO or jingle. The more the announcer
   screams, the more 1986 it gets.

# FEED THE TRADE — why local drafters win when we win

The drafter-facing positioning piece. Not a video script — this is the

---

## The stock-plan lesson (learned the hard way, in the field)

Stock house plans were supposed to eat the local drafter's lunch. They
never did — and the founder ran a stock-homes company and watched why
from the inside:

- **Nobody builds a stock plan.** People use stock plans for
  *inspiration* — they print one off the internet and bring it to a local
  drafter to make it theirs. The catalogue is a mood board, not a product.
- **Stock plans always need local adaptation anyway.** Frost depth, snow
  load, wind zone, the municipal amendment of the week — a frozen PDF
  can't adapt to any of it. The local drafter was never optional.
- **A stock plan sold at stock prices dies the moment the buyer wants one
  change** — then it's custom work at catalogue money, the worst of both.

So stock plans turned out to be the local drafter's **lead generator**:
the client walks in plan-in-hand, already committed, needing exactly the
work only a local can do.

## Rough Drafter is the same relationship at higher octane

We are not the stock catalogue's replacement — we're what the catalogue
wished it was:

- The free-bone tier lets anyone deal themselves a house and walk into a
  drafter's office with a **watermarked ROUGH DRAFTER plan** as their
  inspiration sheet. Every one of those sheets is an ad nobody paid for.
- Unlike a stock JPEG, that inspiration sheet has **real geometry
  underneath** — the drafter opens a working model, not a picture, and
  starts from something that already rebuilds itself.
- Local adaptation stops being a redraw and becomes a parameter change:
  region defaults swap the foundation recipes, heel heights, and
  assemblies, and the local drafter signs off that the recipe fits the
  site. Stock plans *need* local adjustment; ours **are** the local
  adjustment.

## The line

> Stock plans were never the local drafter's competition — they were his
> lead generator. We just build a better lead.

They will still need drafters — but more as a teacher. The tool feeds the
trade instead of eating it. Everybody wins. Synergy.

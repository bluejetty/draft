# RoughDrafter LITE — "The Drag Race" (10-SECOND 80s CUT)

**Tagline: ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!**

Built for one-shot video generators (Sora / Runway / Veo / Kling ~10s max).
Two ways to make it: ONE-PROMPT (single continuous 10s generation) or
4-BEAT (four ~2.5s clips cut together if the single take comes out mushy).

---

## OPTION A — ONE-PROMPT (paste this whole block as the video prompt)

> 1980s toy-commercial aesthetic: grainy VHS videotape, tracking lines,
> oversaturated colors, dusk drag strip with neon grid horizon, comic zoom
> lines. Continuous shot, fast cuts feel: A vertical drag-race light stack
> drops amber-amber-GREEN. An orange 80s wedge sports car with pop-up
> headlights erupts off the line in tire smoke — beside it, a calm 40s man
> in a plaid shirt sits in a rolling office chair at a tiny desk and instead
> draws a neon-green house outline on a chunky laptop, then SLAMS a huge red
> arcade button labeled BUILD HOUSE; the outline extrudes into a 3D white-clay
> house with starburst glints. His office chair rockets past the race car,
> papers streaming like a contrail. At the finish line he is already standing,
> holding a rotating blue hologram house as the car crosses second and two
> wide-eyed kids cheer at the guardrail. Chrome 3D logo slams in:
> "ROUGHDRAFTER LITE — ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!" A tiny dog trots
> across the bottom and sits.

**Audio (add or generate separately):** screaming 80s announcer —
"GREEN LIGHT! HE DRAWS THE HOUSE?! ONE BUTTON BUILDS IT! ROUGHDRAFTER LITE —
ZERO TO PLAN IN FIVE MINUTES!" + synth-rock sting + tire scream +
arcade button THUNK.

## OPTION B — 4-BEAT (four short generations, ~2.5s each)

Use the same GLOBAL STYLE line at the top of each prompt:

> 1980s toy-commercial VHS look: grainy videotape, tracking lines,
> oversaturated colors, dusk drag strip, neon grid horizon. THE DRAFTER —
> calm 40s man, plaid shirt, rolling office chair, tiny desk, chunky laptop.
> THE CAR — orange 80s wedge sports car, pop-up headlights.

**BEAT 1 (0:00–0:02.5) — GREEN + LAUNCH**
Light stack drops amber-amber-GREEN reflected in the driver's visor and the
drafter's glasses; the car erupts in tire smoke, the drafter turns to his
laptop. Comic "GO!!!" burst.
*Announcer: "GREEN LIGHT!"*

**BEAT 2 (0:02.5–0:05) — OUTLINE + BUTTON SLAM**
Laptop screen: neon-green house outline snaps corner-corner-corner, then his
finger SLAMS the huge red arcade BUILD HOUSE button — the outline extrudes
into a 3D white-clay house with starburst glints.
*Announcer: "ONE BUTTON BUILDS THE HOUSE!"*

**BEAT 3 (0:05–0:07.5) — THE OVERTAKE**
Side tracking shot: the office chair rockets past the race car, papers
streaming like a contrail, neon speed lines. Deadpan drafter face.
*Announcer: "HE'S FASTER THAN THE CAR?!"*

**BEAT 4 (0:07.5–0:10) — HOLOGRAM + LOGO**
Finish line: drafter standing with the rotating blue hologram house, car
crossing second, kids cheering. Chrome logo slams in: ROUGHDRAFTER LITE —
ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES! Tiny dog trots across and sits.
*Announcer: "ROUGHDRAFTER LITE! ZERO TO PLAN IN FIVE MINUTES!"*

---

## TIPS

- Generate a storyboard frame per beat first (ChatGPT, same chat for
  consistency), then feed each frame + its beat text as image-to-video.
- If the one-prompt take garbles the laptop screen, generate BEAT 2 alone
  and splice it in — screens are what generators fumble most.
- Ask for "VHS tape wobble" in every clip so the beats cut together seamlessly.

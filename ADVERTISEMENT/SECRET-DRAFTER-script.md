# RoughDrafter LITE — "THE SECRET DRAFTER" (office espionage ad, ~10 seconds)

Same universe as THE PAW and the drag race: semi-realistic 1980s airbrushed-illustration style, halfway between photoreal and cartoon — painted-poster look, oversaturated colors, light VHS grain. Mood: 80s spy-comedy — sneaky, playful, a little paranoid. Mission Impossible-lite synth bassline.

## The spot

**Shot 1 (0–3s) — The grind.**
A 1980s drafting office: rows of drafters hunched over CRT monitors, slow and miserable, command lines and crosshairs, one guy erasing something for the hundredth time. Fluorescent lights hum. In the back corner, OUR drafter (the mustached plaid-shirt guy) glances left, glances right...

**Shot 2 (3–6s) — The secret.**
...and his screen flips to RoughDrafter LITE. A neon-green house outline snaps together in seconds, he slams the red BUILD HOUSE button, the white clay house pops up. He hits export — a DXF slides out and opens on the OFFICE screen looking exactly like everyone else's drawings. He leans back, sips his coffee, smug.

**Shot 3 (6–8s) — The boss.**
The boss walks the aisle behind him, suspicious, coffee mug in hand, squinting at the perfectly ordinary drawing on screen. He sees nothing. He moves on. Our drafter's eyes slide sideways to camera. On the shelf behind him, a tiny dog figurine. The boss stops... turns back slowly...

**Shot 4 (8–10s) — Hard cut to the logo card.**
Chrome 1980s logo on the neon grid and synthwave sun:

> **ROUGHDRAFTER LITE — ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!**
> *Your boss will find out eventually.*

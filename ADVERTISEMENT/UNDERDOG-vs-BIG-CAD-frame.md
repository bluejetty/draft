# Campaign frame: the underdog hero vs. BIG CAD (stored 2026-08)

The narrative spine for the whole ad campaign: RoughDrafter is the scrappy
underdog hero; the villain is BIG CAD — a fictional faceless mega-corp
archetype that the audience recognizes on their own.

## The two characters

**THE HERO — one drafter and a dog.**
- Small, fast, human. Expects a few mistakes (yours and ours).
- Weapon: one paw button. ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES!
- Scuffed armor is part of the costume — the underdog frame makes every
  glitch endearing instead of damning. Heroes are allowed rough edges.

**THE VILLAIN — BIG CAD CORP.**
- Faceless tower. Thousand-dollar subscriptions. Forty-button ribbons.
  Licensing servers. Mandatory updates. "Perfection required."
- Never named as a real company. The archetype does the work.

## Precedents (this is the oldest, best-converting story in advertising)
- Apple "1984" vs IBM
- Mac vs PC
- Avis — "We're #2. We try harder."
- Dollar Shave Club vs Gillette

## Two rules
1. **Fictional villain only.** Parody the "BIG CAD" archetype — never the
   actual Autodesk/Graphisoft name or logo in an ad. The audience fills in
   the blank themselves; that's funnier AND safer.
2. **Punch up, with a wink.** Confident and playful, never bitter or
   whiny. The underdog is having fun; the giant just doesn't get it.

## How existing spots slot into the frame
- DRAG RACE: underdog's beater smokes the corporate garage queen.
- THE PAW / mystery: the hero's weapon, unexplained. The Paw Knows.
- DO IT LIVE parody: drafter melts down under BIG CAD's ribbon maze,
  presses the paw, does it live.
- SECRET DRAFTER: the brotherhood runs on the underdog's tools.

## The line: "The underDOG of CAD."
The user's coinage — the frame in one word. The dog IS the underdog: paw
button, DOG MODE, BONEYARD dog, RUFF DRAFTERS all converge here.
Capitalize the DOG. Also in TAGLINES.md as a support line.

## Tagline pairings
- "ROUGHLY TRUE. NEVER PERFECT." (vs. their "perfection required")
- "A FEW MISTAKES ARE EXPECTED." (the anti-corporate permission slip)
- "ZERO → PLAN IN 5 MINUTES! (or it's free)"

## Long-game note
If the channel outgrows the software (ads-as-content play), the frame
still works — underdog media brand in a space owned by giants. And the
final-boss joke stays on the table: get big enough that BIG CAD buys ad
slots on the underdog's channel.

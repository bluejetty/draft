Follow the attached storyboard image exactly — same salesman, same family,
same office, same field, same dogs, same tank, same sequence of events, in
order. The world and all humans are PHOTOREAL cinematic live-action; the

One continuous 10-second television commercial, smooth camera moves, fast
comedic pacing:

Inside a wood-paneled real estate office, a cheesy salesman in a loud
yellow plaid sport coat gestures grandly at a bulky computer showing
blueprint software, mid-pitch to a young husband and wife with a baby on
her lap. Behind him, a big window overlooks a bare brown dirt field staked
with survey string, just two lonely houses on the far hill. Through the
window, a platoon of cartoon dogs in little green army helmets crests the
hill in marching formation — the baby notices first and points; the
salesman keeps talking, oblivious. A cartoon purple tank rolls over the
hill behind the dogs, kicking real dust off the dirt, and lobs a cartoon
smoke bomb that crashes through the window in a burst of real glass and
billowing pink-and-green cartoon smoke. Two grinning cartoon dogs wrap the
salesman in a gentle squeeze and he slides down out of frame mid-sentence
with a polite POOF — comic, not violent; the family watches, more curious
than afraid. The smoke parts and a glossy cartoon-red arcade button with a
small white bone emblem pops up out of the desk. A cartoon dog takes the
baby's tiny hand and guides it to the button; the baby SLAMS it, thrilled.
Out the broken window, finished photoreal houses pop up out of the dirt
one after another like toast — boing, boing, boing — filling the whole
street in seconds while the dog platoon salutes and the baby claps. End on
a chrome logo card: "ROUGHDRAFTER — ZERO TO HOUSE IN 5 MINUTES!" with "Skip
the pitch." in small text beneath.

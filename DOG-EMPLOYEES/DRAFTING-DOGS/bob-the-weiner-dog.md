# BOB THE WEINER DOG

**Drafting dog. Draft — not on shift.**
Started 31 Aug 2026. Everything here is a first cut and expects to be argued
with.

---

## Who he is

Long. Low. Committed to the bit.

Builds narrow houses that run deep into the lot — **2.5:1 and longer**. It
sounds like a joke and it isn't: it's the shape that fits a narrow city lot,
and it's the shape a long single-run hall is actually good at.

**His one axis: aspect ratio.** Everything he does well and everything he does
badly comes out of that.

## On the sheet

Draws the long axis horizontal, always, even when it fights the sheet.
Signs **BOB**.

---

## What the shape costs him

Worth writing down, because it's the honest half of the personality:

- **Exterior wall per square foot is the worst of any dog.** Long thin houses
  are expensive to build and expensive to heat.
- The spine eats real area — at 4:1 he's spending a lot of the house on
  getting to the end of it.
- The far end is a long way from the front door, so whatever lands there had
  better not be something you use ten times a day.

He's not wrong for a narrow lot. He's wrong for a wide one, and the picker
should probably know that.

---

## What he's good for

Narrow infill lots, row houses, anything where the lot decides the shape
before the client does.

## What he's bad for

Wide lots, where he's paying for exterior wall he didn't need to buy.

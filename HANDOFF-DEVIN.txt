THE DOWNSTREAM COST, MEASURED
-----------------------------
build-stair-openings.spec.js went red on 1.000" in three places. Real,
not tolerance: 3/8" more foundation is 3/8" more rise over 14 risers,
0.027" more per riser, which costs a third of a tread slot and rounds
the run down an inch.

   12.41 slots        ->  12.366 slots
   OPEN_LEN_FT  125"  ->  124"
   L stair run 2 55"  ->  54"
   a -5.05 threshold that sat 1/100 clear of the old answer, rewritten
     as (-5 - FINISH_FT) so it states intent instead of a magic number

THE OPENING FOLLOWS THE WALKING LINE CONTINUOUSLY, NOT IN WHOLE TREADS.
I got this wrong once today -- reasoned that a run moving in 10" treads
could not produce a 1" delta, and dismissed the foundation as the cause
when it was exactly the cause. Do not repeat it.
